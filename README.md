Hi, I'm Mrinaal! 👋

I work independently on **[AI research](https://aroramrinaal.com/ai)** full-time, focused on **AI alignment, AI safety, multilingual models and agent evaluation**. Along the way, I've worked hands-on with language-model training and post-training, reinforcement learning, mechanistic interpretability and multi-agent systems. I also maintain <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aroramrinaal/aroramrinaal/main/assets/profile-readme/india-lens-419ebefa85c9.png" width="28" align="absmiddle" alt="" /> [isindiakeepingup.com](https://isindiakeepingup.com/), which tracks India's LLM release pace against the US, China, Singapore and South Korea.

Previously, I was a Software Engineer at <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aroramrinaal/aroramrinaal/main/assets/profile-readme/ohara-f5c8c916d312.gif" width="16" align="texttop" alt="" /> [Ohara](https://ohara.ai), where I owned the core frontend, shipped it as a live Base miniapp and built the integration pipeline that let AI-generated apps run inside Base. Before that, I interned at <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aroramrinaal/aroramrinaal/main/assets/profile-readme/ok-health-134493e20bbb.gif" width="16" align="texttop" alt="" /> [OK Health](https://okhealth.ai/), building their embeddable AI chatbot.

I graduated Summa Cum Laude with a B.S. in Data Science from <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aroramrinaal/aroramrinaal/main/assets/profile-readme/asu-mark-1c4971818fe1.svg" width="48" align="absmiddle" alt="" /> <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aroramrinaal/aroramrinaal/main/assets/profile-readme/arizona-885198a83c4b.gif" width="32" align="texttop" alt="" /> Arizona State University in 2025. During college I won 6+ hackathons, including at Harvard and Yale, and built viral websites like <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aroramrinaal/aroramrinaal/main/assets/profile-readme/your-wrapped.png" width="16" align="texttop" alt="" /> [Your-Wrapped](https://your-wrapped.aroramrinaal.com/), <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aroramrinaal/aroramrinaal/main/assets/profile-readme/iammusic.png" width="16" align="texttop" alt="" /> [iammusic2024.com](https://iammusic2024.com/), and <picture><img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aroramrinaal/aroramrinaal/main/assets/profile-readme/world-fan-arena-football-vector-85458f569f99.svg" width="18" align="texttop" alt="" /></picture>&nbsp;[World Fan Arena](https://aroramrinaal.com/world-fan-arena/rooms), which together reached 200k+ unique visitors.
